## Jurisdictional Arbitrage
Northern Europe is a region of small but sovereign jurisdictions with high regulatory clarity. That clarity enables multi-country collateral structures with lower legal friction than Central or Southern Europe.

## Sammanfattning

Northern Europe’s private asset-based lending (ABL) market is ascending in 2026 due to structural misalignment in traditional bank lending, creating a premium opportunity for lenders underwriting operating assets in constrained yet high-growth mid-market sectors. Jurisdictional arbitrage—exploiting sovereign clarity and post-2024 UK regulatory shifts—positions the region as the OECD’s most asymmetrically advantageous lending environment.
