## Why Liquidity Has Become the Primary Strategic Constraint



UHNW portfolios once sourced liquidity from three dominant channels: public equities, high grade debt, and cash instruments within the traditional banking system.  

All three have weakened simultaneously.  



Public equities deliver liquidity, but not stable liquidity.  
  
High grade sovereign and corporate debt has lost the yield buffer that once justified its defensive role.  
   
And cash inside the banking system, while functionally liquid, carries both counterparty concentration risk and the opportunity cost of underutilization.  



This convergence has created an asymmetry.  

Portfolios that appear diversified are often concentrated in liquidity behavior.  

When markets compress, they compress in unison.  

When liquidity is needed most, it is most expensive.  



This is the vulnerability UHNW families now seek to eliminate.  

Structural liquidity failure is not a drawdown.  

It is a paralysis event.
