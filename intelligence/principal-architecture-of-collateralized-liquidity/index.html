Tier One: Primary Operating Assets These include revenue-generating divisions, mission-critical capital equipment, or contracted cashflows. Tier Two: Secondary Hard Assets Inventory, receivables, non-core equipment, transport fleets, and physical infrastructure. These assets form the backbone of most Asset-Based Lending structures.
